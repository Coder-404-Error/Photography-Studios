03 / HOW WE WORK

From brief
to final frame.

A simple four-step process — built for clarity and craft.

STEP 01

Discovery & Brief

We start with a short call to understand your vision — species, location, mood, deliverable and timeline. Everything else flows from this conversation.

STEP 02

Location & Planning

Based on the species you want to capture, we recommend the best national park, season and time of day. Permits and logistics are handled end-to-end.

STEP 03

On Location & Shoot

We spend the time on the ground — patiently waiting for the right moment. Multiple shoot days, silent approaches and long telephoto work are all part of the process.

STEP 04

Edit & Delivery

Every frame is hand-graded in our signature cinematic tone. You receive curated selects, high-resolution files, and social-ready crops — all delivered in 2-3 weeks.
