Your privacy matters. We only collect what we need to run our studio — nothing more. This policy explains exactly what we gather, why, and how you can control it.

01 Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website, book a session, or subscribe to our journal, we collect the following:

  • Contact details — name, email, phone number you provide via our forms.
  • Project information — service details, briefs, reference images you share.
  • Technical data — IP address, browser type, device information, and anonymous usage data.
  • Payment information — processed securely via third-party providers. We never store card details.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use your data strictly for the following purposes:

Responding to Enquiries

To reply to your messages, quotes and booking requests.

Project Delivery

To plan, execute and deliver your photography or film work.

Service Updates

To send important updates about your project or our services.

Newsletter

Only if you subscribe — and you can unsubscribe any time.

We never sell your data. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. Your information stays between us.

03 Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected. Project files and client records are typically retained for 3 years after project completion, in case of future requests or reprints.

Newsletter data is kept until you unsubscribe. Website analytics data is retained in anonymised form.

04 Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws (including India's DPDP Act and GDPR for EU visitors), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Delete your data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Object to certain processing activities.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for newsletter or marketing.

05 Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures — SSL encryption, secure servers, and restricted access — to protect your data. While no system is 100% secure, we take this responsibility seriously.

Fair refunds, always. If something isn't right, we'd rather fix it — but when a refund is needed, our policy is transparent and simple.

01 Refund Eligibility

Refunds are available under the following circumstances:

  • Cancellation made more than 14 days before the shoot date.
  • We cancel a project and cannot offer a reschedule.
  • Duplicate payments or billing errors.
  • Service not delivered as described in the confirmed brief.

02 Non-Refundable Items

  • Shoots already completed or in progress.
  • Delivered final files (once approved and downloaded).
  • Travel, permits, or third-party costs already incurred.
  • Rush fees and priority delivery charges.

03 Refund Timeline

Stage Action Time

Review We review and respond 3–5 business days
Processing Refund issued to original payment method 7–10 business days

01 What You Get

Unless otherwise stated in your quote, all our deliverables (photographs, film, designs) come with the following rights:

  • Print rights for personal or internal business use.

02 What We Retain

GM Studio retains copyright ownership of all images and films. You receive a broad usage license — not the copyright itself — unless we sign a separate written agreement transferring full rights.

We also retain the right to showcase the work in our portfolio, on social media, and in marketing materials — unless you specifically request confidentiality in writing.

03 Restrictions

Under our standard license, you may not:

  • Resell the images or films as standalone assets.
  • License them to third parties without written consent.
  • Use them in AI training datasets.
